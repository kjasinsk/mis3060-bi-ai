# HW3 Analysis

**Business question:** Do the executive changes appear to precede or follow earnings announcements? Is there a pattern across the five companies, or does it vary?

Across the five companies, most executive changes were filed **before** the nearest earnings announcement: 15 events came before earnings and 6 came after, with none in the same week. The pattern varies a lot by company. All seven of Walmart's events came before earnings, driven by one leadership reshuffle filed on January 16, 2026, about a month before its February earnings release and timed to John Furner taking over as CEO on February 1. Apple and JPMorgan also filed their changes 10–27 days before earnings. Microsoft is the opposite: all three of its events came 15–40 days *after* earnings. NVIDIA was split, with four events before and three after. The fact that nothing landed in the same week as earnings suggests these companies may deliberately keep leadership news separate from their results, so one doesn't overshadow the other. With only 21 events, many of them routine board appointments tied to annual-meeting timing rather than to results, this is a small sample, and it shows company-specific timing habits more than a reliable market-wide pattern.
