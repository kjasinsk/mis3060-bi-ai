# HW3 Analysis

**Business question:** Do the executive changes precede or follow earnings announcements? Is there a pattern across the five companies?

Based on my data, most executive changes were filed before earnings: 19 of the 27 events came before the nearest earnings announcement, 6 came after, and 2 were in the same week. The pattern depends a lot on the company, since all 4 of JPMorgan's events and 7 of Walmart's 9 came before earnings (mostly Walmart's January 16 leadership shake-up and JPMorgan naming new Co-Presidents three weeks before its Q2 results), while both of Microsoft's board changes came after earnings and Apple and NVIDIA were split (4 before and 2 after for each). The only same-week events were Walmart's CEO succession (Doug McMillon retiring and John Furner taking over), filed six days before its Q3 earnings, so even very big leadership news sometimes lands close to results. Apple's biggest change, Tim Cook moving from CEO to Executive Chair with John Ternus as the next CEO, was filed 10 days before earnings. With only 27 events, and many of them coming in batches from a single filing, I don't think there is enough data to say companies time leadership news around earnings on purpose.
