# HW3 Analysis

**Business question:** Do the executive changes precede or follow earnings announcements? Is there a pattern across the five companies?

Based on my data, most of the executive changes happened before earnings, with 15 of the 21 events filed before the nearest earnings announcement, 6 filed after, and none in the same week. It really depends on the company though, since Walmart had all 7 of its events before earnings, mostly because of one big leadership shake-up on January 16, 2026, right before John Furner took over as CEO. Microsoft was the opposite, with all 3 of its changes coming 2 to 6 weeks after earnings, while NVIDIA was split (4 before, 3 after). I thought it was interesting that nothing landed in the same week as earnings, which makes me think companies try to keep leadership news separate from their results. Since there are only 21 events and a lot of them are just board appointments, I don't think this is enough to say there is a real pattern across all companies.
